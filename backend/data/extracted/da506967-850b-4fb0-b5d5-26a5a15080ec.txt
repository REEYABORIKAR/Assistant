1. Executive Summary & Strategic Rationale
The proposed HR Management System (HRMS) is a full-stack web and mobile application designed to centralize and automate core
human resources operations. The strategic rationale for this system is to replace fragmented HR processes with a unified, API-driven
platform that supports end-to-end employee lifecycle management—from recruitment and onboarding to payroll, performance, and
offboarding. By implementing strict Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) and secure authentication, the system ensures data privacy
and operational integrity. The platform is designed to be production-ready, scalable, and compliant with banking industry standards,
providing a robust foundation for managing organizational headcount, financial payroll operations, and employee engagement.
2. Business Objectives & Success Metrics
**Business Objectives:**
1. Centralize employee master data, departments, and organizational structures.
2. Automate end-to-end workflows including recruitment, onboarding, attendance, leave, and payroll.
3. Ensure secure, role-based access for Super Admin, HR/Admin, Manager, Recruiter, Employee, and Finance/Payroll users.
4. Provide comprehensive analytics and reporting for HR, Finance, and Management.
**Success Metrics:**
- 100% coverage of core HR modules (Onboarding, Payroll, Recruitment, Performance, Offboarding).
- Successful implementation of configurable approval chains for HR, managers, finance, and admins.
- Achieve 99.99% uptime for the centralized backend API and web/mobile interfaces.
- Complete audit trail generation for all critical financial and employee data modifications.
3. Scope & Operational Boundaries (In/Out of Scope)
**In Scope:**
- **Core HR:** Employee master data, departments, designations, branches, onboarding/offboarding, profiles, document
management.
- **Time & Attendance:** Attendance, shift/roster management, leave/holiday management, WFH/remote-work requests, timesheets.
- **Payroll & Finance:** Payroll processing, salary structures, deductions, bonuses, reimbursements, payslips, tax/statutory info,
expense management, travel requests.
- **Recruitment:** Applicant tracking, job postings, candidate pipeline, interviews, offers, joining management.
- **Performance & Development:** Goals/KPIs, appraisals, feedback, training and learning.
- **Administration:** Asset management, grievances, announcements, notifications, policies, HR letters/certificates, surveys,
self-service portals, dashboards/reports.
- **Technical Infrastructure:** Secure authentication (JWT/MFA-ready), RBAC, relational database, file storage, notification service,
background jobs, logging, monitoring, encryption, backups, API integrations.
**Out of Scope:**
- The source document does not explicitly define out-of-scope items. However, specific third-party ERP integrations (beyond generic
API integrations) and physical hardware procurement are not detailed in the excerpt and are considered out of scope for this initial
BRD phase pending stakeholder clarification.

4. Key Stakeholders & Governance Personas
The system defines the following governance personas and stakeholders:
- **Super Admin:** Full system configuration, master-data management, and organizational settings.
- **HR/Admin:** Management of employee lifecycle, recruitment, performance, and HR policies.
- **Manager:** Approval workflows for leave, expenses, and performance reviews; team analytics.
- **Recruiter:** Management of job postings, candidate pipelines, and interview scheduling.
- **Employee:** Self-service portal for leave requests, timesheets, expense claims, and profile management.
- **Finance/Payroll:** Management of salary structures, payroll processing, deductions, and statutory compliance.

**Assumptions:**
- The system will operate on a centralized backend API with a relational database.
- Users will access the system via modern, responsive Web, Android, and iOS interfaces.
- Notification services (email/SMS/push) will be integrated with third-party providers.
**Operational Constraints:**
- **Compliance:** The system must adhere to banking industry standards, including PCI-DSS (for any payment-related data), SOX
(for financial reporting integrity), AML/KYC (for identity verification in onboarding), and ISO 27001 (for information security
management).
- **Security:** Data privacy, encryption at rest and in transit, and secure document access are mandatory.
- **Scalability:** The architecture must support background jobs and monitoring to handle high-volume payroll and attendance
processing.
7. Risk & Compliance Baseline
**Compliance Frameworks:**
- **PCI-DSS:** Ensure any payment card data (if handled in reimbursements) is encrypted and access-restricted.
- **SOX:** Implement strict audit trails and segregation of duties for payroll and financial approvals to ensure financial reporting
accuracy.
- **AML/KYC:** Integrate identity verification steps in the onboarding workflow to comply with anti-money laundering regulations.
- **ISO 27001:** Establish an information security management system (ISMS) covering risk assessment, access control, and
incident response.
**NFR Benchmarks:**
- **Transaction Atomicity:** Payroll and financial transactions must be atomic to prevent partial updates.
- **Audit Immutability:** Audit logs must be tamper-proof and immutable.
- **Uptime:** The system must achieve 99.99% uptime for critical HR and payroll operations.
**Risk Mitigation:**
- Implement MFA-ready architecture to mitigate unauthorized access risks.
- Use configurable approval chains to enforce segregation of duties.
- Regular backups and monitoring to ensure data recovery and system stability.
