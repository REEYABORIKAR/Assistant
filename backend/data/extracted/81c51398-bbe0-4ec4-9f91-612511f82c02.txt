Client Business Idea 
1.1 Business Objective 
Northfield Retail Group operates 46 physical stores across the western region and currently has no direct-to-
consumer digital sales channel. Management has approved funding for NovaCart, a customer-facing e-commerce 
platform that will allow shoppers to browse, purchase and track products online while giving store operations staff a 
single system to manage catalogue, inventory and fulfilment. The platform is expected to become the company's 
primary growth channel over the next three fiscal years. 
1.2 Current Business Problem 
Sales today are confined to physical footfall, which caps revenue to store catchment areas and store operating hours. 
Customers who want to check stock, compare products or place an order outside of store hours have no channel to 
do so, and the company has already lost measurable market share to regional competitors that operate online 
storefronts. Customer service also fields a high volume of phone enquiries about order status because there is no 
self-service tracking capability. 
1.3 Expected Business Value 
● A new digital revenue stream projected to contribute 18% of total company revenue within 24 months of 
launch. 
● Reduction in inbound customer service call volume through self-service order tracking and order history. 
● Improved inventory visibility across the store network through a unified product catalogue. 
● A foundation for future loyalty, personalisation and marketplace initiatives. 
1.4 Business Goals 
● Launch a functioning e-commerce storefront within two fiscal quarters. 
● Provide customers a self-service channel for registration, ordering and tracking. 
● Give store operations a single administrative system for catalogue and order management.

4. Business Requirement Document (BRD) 
4.1. Introduction 
4.1.1 Purpose 
This document describes the business requirements for developing an e-commerce website. It helps the 
client and development team understand the project goals and expected features. It also acts as a reference 
throughout the project lifecycle. 
4.1.2 Business Need 
The client currently sells products only through physical stores. Customers cannot shop online or track their 
orders. The new website will provide an online shopping platform to improve customer experience. 
4.1.3 Project Overview 
This is an online shopping website where customers can register, search products, place orders, and track 
deliveries. Store administrators can manage products, inventory, and customer orders through an admin 
panel. 
4.2. Business Objectives 
The main objective is to build a secure and user-friendly e-commerce website. The system should increase 
online sales, improve customer satisfaction, and reduce manual work. Customers should be able to complete 
purchases easily. 
Objective ID Objective 
OBJ-01 Enable customers to create and securely access an account 
OBJ-02 Provide fast and relevant product discovery 
OBJ-03 Provide a low-friction cart and checkout experience 
OBJ-04 Give customers self-service visibility of order status and history 
4.3. Problem Statement 
Customers currently have to visit physical stores to purchase products. They cannot check product 
availability or order status online. This leads to reduced customer convenience and limits business growth. A 
customer wishing to buy a product today must visit a physical store during trading hours, locate the item on 
the shelf or ask staff to check the paper catalogue, and pay at a till. There is no way to check stock, compare 
products or place an order remotely.

5.7. Use Case Specifications 
UC-001 — Customer Registration (BR-001) 
 Actor: Customer 
 Precondition: The user is not already registered. 
 Main Flow: Customer submits email/mobile number and password → system validates uniqueness 
→ account created in unverified state → OTP sent → customer enters OTP → account verified. 
 Exception: Duplicate email/mobile is rejected with an error message. 
 Postcondition: A new, verified customer account exists. 
UC-002 — Customer Login (BR-001) 
 Actor: Customer 
 Precondition: Customer holds a verified account. 
 Main Flow: Customer enters credentials → system validates → session established. 
 Exception: Three consecutive failed attempts lock the account temporarily. 
 Postcondition: Customer is authenticated with an active session. 
UC-003 — Search and View Product (BR-002) 
 Actor: Customer 
 Precondition: Product catalogue is populated. 
 Main Flow: Customer searches by keyword or filters by category/price/availability → system 
returns matching products → customer selects a product → detail page displays price, images, 
description and stock status. 
 Exception: No matching products returns a "no results" message. 
 Postcondition: Customer has the information needed to decide whether to purchase. 
UC-004 — Manage Cart and Checkout (BR-003) 
 Actor: Customer 
 Precondition: Customer is authenticated and viewing a product or existing cart. 
 Main Flow: Customer adds/updates/removes cart items → proceeds to checkout → provides 
delivery address and payment details → payment is authorised → order is confirmed. 
 Exception: Insufficient stock blocks the cart update; incomplete address blocks checkout; a declined 
payment blocks order creation. 
 Postcondition: A confirmed, paid order exists. 
UC-005 — Track and Review Orders (BR-004) 
 Actor: Customer 
 Precondition: Customer has placed at least one order.

 Main Flow: Customer views real-time status of an active order or lists past orders in order history; 
may cancel an order while it is "Processing". 
 Exception: Cancellation is blocked once the order has shipped. 
 Postcondition: Customer has current visibility of order status and full order history.

9. Acceptance Criteria  
AC ID Story Scenario Gherkin 
AC-001 US-001 Successful registration 
Given a visitor on the registration page, When they submit a valid email, 
mobile number and password, Then an account is created in an 
unverified state and an OTP is sent. 
AC-002 US-001 Duplicate account 
rejected 
Given a visitor enters an email already registered, When they submit 
the registration form, Then the system rejects the request with a 
duplicate-account error and does not create a new account. 
AC-003 US-002 Successful OTP 
verification 
Given an unverified account, When the customer enters the correct OTP 
within the validity window, Then the account status changes to verified. 
AC-004 US-002 Expired or incorrect 
OTP 
Given an unverified account, When the customer enters an incorrect or 
expired OTP, Then verification is rejected and the customer may request 
a new OTP. 
AC-005 US-003 Successful login 
Given a verified account, When the customer enters the correct 
email/mobile and password, Then the customer is logged in and 
redirected to the homepage. 
AC-006 US-003 Invalid credentials 
Given a verified account, When the customer enters an incorrect 
password three consecutive times, Then the account is temporarily 
locked and the customer is notified. 
AC-007 US-004 Password reset 
request 
Given a registered customer on the forgot-password page, When they 
submit their registered email, Then a reset link is sent and expires after 
30 minutes.

AC ID Story Scenario Gherkin 
AC-008 US-004 Reset link reuse 
Given a customer has already used a password reset link, When they 
attempt to use the same link again, Then the system rejects it as 
expired. 
AC-009 US-005 Keyword search 
returns results 
Given products exist matching a keyword, When the customer searches 
that keyword, Then matching products are displayed ranked by 
relevance. 
AC-010 US-005 No matching results 
Given no products match a keyword, When the customer searches that 
keyword, Then a 'no results found' message with suggested categories is 
displayed. 
AC-011 US-006 Filter by price range 
Given search results are displayed, When the customer applies a 
minimum and maximum price filter, Then only products within that 
range remain visible. 
AC-012 US-006 Filter with no matches 
Given a customer applies filters with no matching products, When the 
filter is applied, Then the system displays a message indicating no 
products match the selected filters. 
AC-013 US-007 Product detail displays 
correctly 
Given a customer selects a product from search results, When the 
product detail page loads, Then price, images, description and stock 
status are displayed accurately. 
AC-014 US-007 Out-of-stock product 
Given a product has zero available stock, When the customer views its 
detail page, Then the Add to Cart action is disabled and an out-of-stock 
label is shown. 
AC-015 US-008 Add in-stock item to 
cart 
Given a product with available stock, When the customer selects Add to 
Cart, Then the item appears in the cart with quantity one. 
AC-016 US-008 Add out-of-stock item 
blocked 
Given a product with zero stock, When the customer attempts to add it 
to cart, Then the system prevents the action and displays an error. 
AC-017 US-009 Update cart quantity 
Given an item is in the cart, When the customer changes its quantity to 
a valid number within available stock, Then the cart total recalculates 
immediately. 
AC-018 US-009 Remove cart item Given an item is in the cart, When the customer selects Remove, Then 
the item is removed and the cart total recalculates. 
AC-019 US-010 Valid checkout 
submission 
Given a cart with at least one item, When the customer provides a valid 
delivery address and proceeds, Then the system advances to the 
payment step. 
AC-020 US-010 Incomplete address 
Given the customer leaves a mandatory address field blank, When they 
attempt to proceed to payment, Then the system displays a validation 
error and blocks progression. 
AC-021 US-011 Successful payment 
Given valid payment details are submitted at the gateway, When the 
payment is authorised, Then an order is created and a confirmation is 
shown to the customer. 
AC-022 US-011 Declined payment 
Given the payment gateway declines a transaction, When the decline 
response is received, Then no order is created and the customer is 
prompted to retry with different payment details. 
AC-023 US-012 View current order 
status 
Given a customer has an active order, When they open order tracking, 
Then the current status and last updated timestamp are displayed. 
AC-024 US-012 No active orders Given a customer has no active orders, When they open order tracking, 
Then a message indicating no active orders is displayed. 
AC-025 US-013 View order history list Given a customer has previously placed orders, When they open order 
history, Then all past orders are listed with date, total and status. 
AC-026 US-013 Empty order history Given a customer has never placed an order, When they open order 
history, Then a message indicating no past orders is displayed. 
AC-027 US-014 Cancel while 
processing 
Given an order is in 'Processing' status, When the customer selects 
Cancel Order, Then the order status changes to 'Cancelled' and any 
payment hold is released. 
AC-028 US-014 Cancel blocked after 
dispatch 
Given an order has moved to 'Shipped' status, When the customer 
attempts to cancel, Then the system blocks the action and explains that 
the order can no longer be cancelled.
